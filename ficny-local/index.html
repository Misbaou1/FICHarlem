Qur’an & Islamic Studies for Every Generation

At FUTA Islamic Center, we believe that the knowledge of the Qur’an and Islamic teachings should be accessible to everyone — from young children to adults and elders. Our well-structured programs include Qur’an memorization (Hifz), Tajweed classes, and foundational Islamic studies. Guided by experienced and compassionate teachers, we strive to create an engaging and nurturing environment that fosters both spiritual growth and moral excellence. Whether you are beginning your journey with the Qur’an or seeking to deepen your understanding, our doors are open for all.

Learning at our center isn’t just about reading; it’s about living the values of the Qur’an in everyday life — with kindness, patience, humility, and faith.

Building a Stronger, United Community

FUTA Islamic Center is more than a place of worship — it’s a hub for community service, social support, and Islamic brotherhood. Our goal is to strengthen the bond among community members by organizing regular events, youth activities, family counseling sessions, charity drives, and educational seminars.

We encourage active participation from all — men, women, elders, and youth — in shaping a vibrant and spiritually grounded environment. Through unity, mutual support, and sincere intention, we hope to create a community that upholds the principles of Islam while addressing real-life challenges with compassion and collaboration.

From Jummah prayers to community iftars, and from Zakat distribution to weekend schools, every initiative reflects our commitment to serve the people and please Allah (SWT).
