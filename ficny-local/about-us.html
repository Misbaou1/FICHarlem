About FUTA Islamic Center

Building Faith, Knowledge, and Unity within Our Community

FUTA Islamic Center is more than just a place of worship — it's a thriving hub for spiritual growth, community support, and Islamic education. Located in the heart of our community, we serve as a beacon of light for all Muslims seeking to strengthen their faith, expand their knowledge, and connect with one another. Our center offers a wide range of programs and services that cater to the needs of individuals and families alike.

Whether you're looking for a place to pray, learn about Islam, or engage in community service, FUTA Islamic Center is here to support you on your spiritual journey. We are proud to welcome people from all backgrounds and walks of life, united by our shared values of compassion, charity, and faith.

Our Mission:

At FUTA Islamic Center, our mission is to create a welcoming space where Muslims of all ages and backgrounds can come together to worship, learn, and serve. We aim to inspire individuals to live their lives in accordance with the teachings of Islam, fostering a sense of brotherhood and sisterhood within the wider community. Through our educational programs, charity initiatives, and community events, we strive to build a stronger, more united society based on Islamic principles.

Our Vision:

Our vision is to be a leading Islamic institution in the United States, known for its commitment to providing high-quality education, fostering interfaith dialogue, and promoting social justice. We aspire to create a center where Muslims can deepen their connection to Allah, strengthen their understanding of Islam, and contribute positively to society at large.

What We Offer:

Daily Prayers & Jummah

Join us for daily prayers and weekly Jummah services. Our mosque provides a serene environment for worship and spiritual reflection.

Educational Programs

From Quran memorization to Islamic studies, we offer structured learning for all ages to grow in knowledge and deen.

Community & Social Services

We host Ramadan Iftars, Eid events, charity drives, and family support programs to uplift our local community.

Youth & Family Programs

Our programs empower youth through leadership and mentorship while supporting families with faith-based resources.
