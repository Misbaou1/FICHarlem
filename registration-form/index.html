Rabbi zidni 'ilma — "My Lord, increase me in knowledge." (Qur'an 20:114)

At FUTA Islamic School, we strive to cultivate a nurturing, respectful, and spiritually enriching environment for every student. These rules and expectations are built not to restrict, but to uplift our young learners, support our educators, and reassure every parent that their child's well-being is our priority.

Core Values & Conduct

1. Character First — Adab Before 'Ilm. Students are expected to carry themselves with Islamic manners at all times, respecting peers, teachers, elders, and the school space. Disruptive or inappropriate behavior will be addressed with wisdom, consistency, and fairness.

2. Respect for Teachers & Elders. Teachers are honored guides. Students must listen, respond respectfully, and follow appropriate instructions.

3. Be Prepared. Students should arrive ready to learn, with all necessary supplies and a heart open for knowledge.

4. Zero Tolerance for Violence or Inappropriate Language. Fighting, bullying, threats, or foul and abusive language are strictly prohibited. Serious incidents may result in immediate suspension and a parent/administration meeting. Repeated or severe violations may result in termination of enrollment.

5. Spiritual Readiness — Wudu & Salat. Students should arrive with wudu whenever reasonably possible and be prepared to pray on time. Salat is central to our mission and is observed with reverence.

6. Calm and Orderly Movement. Running, shouting, or reckless behavior in the building is not allowed. Courteous conduct helps maintain peace and safety.

Dress Code — Dignity in Appearance

Boys: clean, modest kaftan and kufi. The kaftan is not restricted to a particular color. Hats, hoods, sunglasses, earrings, or distracting/excessive hairstyles are not permitted during school.

Girls: loose-fitting, full-coverage abaya with hijab. The abaya does not have to be black and may be another modest, appropriate color. Wigs and nail polish are not permitted.

All students must dress in a clean and modest manner that reflects the spirit and dignity of Islam.

Electronics & Emergency Contact

If a child is dropped off by a parent or guardian, we ask that the parent keep the child's phone and return it at pickup. If a student arrives independently and needs a phone for travel or safety, the phone will be collected upon arrival, safely stored, and returned at dismissal.

Emergency contacts — School Office: 646-427-7853 / 917-969-6494. Masjid email: futaislamicharlem@gmail.com

School Hours, Breaks & Attendance

Winter: 9:45 AM – 1:30 PM, no scheduled break. Summer: 9:45 AM – 12:30 PM, 10-minute break between sessions.

During the 10-minute breaks, students must remain inside the building. No student may leave the building during the school day. Students must come with the food/snack they will need.

Punctuality is essential. Students arriving after 10:00 AM will not be admitted to class without an accepted documented reason, such as a medical appointment, unless prior approval has been given by administration.

Parents must communicate absences and extended travel. An entire month of absence without prior notice or communication may be considered a voluntary withdrawal.

Engagement, Homework & Evaluation

Active participation is expected. Repeated disruption may result in removal from class, parent notification, and possible suspension.

Homework represents 50% of the final grade. Late submissions will be accepted only with a valid reason and appropriate communication with the teacher.

Parents may contact the school to schedule meetings with teachers outside class hours. Parent-teacher conferences and progress reports may also be scheduled during the school year.

Fees, Tuition & Enrollment

Registration fee: $50 per student, paid once at enrollment for the school year and separate from tuition.

Tuition: 1 student $60 per month · 2 students $100 total per month · 3 students $120 total per month · each additional student beyond three $40 per month.

At enrollment, families choosing the monthly plan pay the one-time registration fee for each student plus three months of tuition upfront. These cover the first month and the final two months. The family then pays regular monthly tuition for the remaining nine months.

Families who pay the full 12 months of tuition at enrollment receive a 5% discount on tuition. Registration remains separate and is not discounted.

After the initial enrollment payment, each remaining monthly payment is due on the 1st. If payment is not received by the 5th and no arrangement has been approved, the student may not attend class until the account is current.

Tuition fees are non-refundable once the applicable period has begun. Requests for reductions or credits because of missed classes or non-attendance cannot normally be accommodated. Families facing genuine financial hardship are encouraged to speak privately with administration before payment becomes overdue.

An entire month of absence without prior notice or communication may be treated as voluntary withdrawal. Re-enrollment requires a new registration and is subject to available space.

Student Safety, Belongings & Support

FUTA Islamic School takes reasonable precautions to protect students while under school supervision. Parents must inform the school of relevant medical, allergy, behavioral, or safety concerns.

Students are responsible for personal belongings. Please label items clearly. The school cannot guarantee recovery or replacement of lost or damaged items.

If your child has medical, learning, developmental, or behavioral needs, please inform administration confidentially so the school can determine what reasonable support can be provided.

Parent Conduct & Collaboration

Parents and guardians are expected to model Islamic values when interacting with teachers, administrators, students, and families. Concerns should be raised respectfully through appropriate school channels.

Media Consent

Photos or short videos may occasionally be taken during school activities for internal school use or FUTA Islamic Center communications. Parents who do not wish their child to appear should notify the school in writing.

Acknowledgment & Agreement

Violation of school rules may result in corrective action, parent conferences, suspension, or termination of enrollment depending on the seriousness or repetition of the violation.
